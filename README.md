# 🌍 AirGuard AI

## AI-Powered Community Climate Intelligence & Pollution Response Platform

> **Build with AI: Code for Communities — Second Edition**  
> **Track 2 — Clean Air & Climate Resilience**

AirGuard AI is an AI-powered community climate intelligence platform designed to help communities and local authorities detect potential pollution hotspots, understand environmental risks, and support faster, data-driven climate action.

The platform combines **citizen-generated reports, environmental sensor data, weather information, satellite/geospatial data, and AI-powered analysis** into a unified environmental intelligence system.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Major cities and communities often face environmental pollution events that are difficult to identify and understand at a hyper-local level.

Environmental information is usually distributed across multiple sources, including:

- Citizen complaints and observations
- Environmental sensors
- Weather and meteorological data
- Satellite and geospatial information
- Historical environmental data

Because these sources are fragmented, communities and authorities may struggle to:

- Detect emerging pollution hotspots
- Understand the possible causes and context
- Identify affected areas
- Prioritize environmental incidents
- Coordinate timely responses
- Communicate environmental risks clearly to citizens

AirGuard AI addresses this gap by bringing these signals together into a single AI-assisted platform.
